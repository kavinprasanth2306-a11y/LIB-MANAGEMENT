Applied ADTs:
  • Custom HashTable: O(1) Book & Member lookup with separate chaining collision buckets.

  • Sorting Suite: MergeSort and QuickSort for catalog ranking (Title, Year, Borrows, Availability).

  • Min-Heap & Max-Heap: O(1) top tracking for earliest overdue returns & trending titles.
  • B-Tree Simulation: Multi-key block indexing simulation for secondary storage.
