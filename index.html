Curriculum Course Outcomes (CO)

Mapping of Linear, Non-Linear, Searching, Sorting, and Tree Data Structures applied in this project.

CO 1 to CO 4
CO 1 Linear Structures

Stacks, Queues, Deques & Priority Queues

Applied ADTs:
  • Stack ADT (Stack): Action Audit Log & transaction rollbacks (undo operations).
  • CircularQueue & Deque: Return drop-box buffering with modulo wrap-around & VIP front/rear scheduling.
  • PriorityQueue / Queue: Out-of-stock reservation and waitlist handling with FIFO urgency.
  • Formula Evaluator: Infix-to-postfix stack calculation for overdue fines.
CO 2 Linked Lists & Search

Singly, Doubly, Circular Lists & Basic Search

Applied ADTs:
  • DoublyLinkedList (DLL): Bidirectional recently viewed books navigator (Prev <-> Next).
  • SinglyLinkedList: Borrower transaction history log linked chronologically.
  • CircularLinkedList: Featured books continuous showcase carousel.
  • Search Comparison: Sequential scan vs. divide-and-conquer binary search step metrics.
CO 3 Hashing & Sorting

Hash Tables, Collision Resolution & Sorting Suite

Applied ADTs:
  • Custom HashTable: O(1) Book & Member lookup with separate chaining collision buckets.
  • Linear Probing & Chaining: Real-time collision inspection in the live memory visualizer.
  • Interpolation Search: Numerical probe calculation for uniform ISBN indices.
  • Sorting Suite: MergeSort and QuickSort for catalog ranking (Title, Year, Borrows, Availability).
CO 4 Hierarchical Structures

Trees, Rotations, Tries, Heaps & B-Trees

Applied ADTs:
  • Trie (Prefix Tree): O(L) real-time autocomplete search for titles and authors.
  • AVL Tree & BST: Balanced catalog indexing with LL, RR, LR, RL tree rotations.
  • Min-Heap & Max-Heap: O(1) top tracking for earliest overdue returns & trending titles.
  • B-Tree Simulation: Multi-key block indexing simulation for secondary storage.
